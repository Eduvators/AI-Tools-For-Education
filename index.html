Eduvators Student Privacy & Classroom Safety Guide

                
            

            

                

                    
1. Keep Student Personal Information Private

                    
Teachers and students should never type student names, grades, home addresses, or private school records into public AI tools. Whenever possible, sign in with your official school email account so your school privacy agreement protects your work.

                

                

                    
⚠ 2. Always Double Check AI Answers

                    
AI tools can sometimes make up facts or sound confident when they are wrong. Students and teachers are always in charge of checking facts, reading original sources, watching out for unfair stereotypes, and putting final work into their own authentic voice.

                

                

                    
3. Built for Every Learner (Accessibility)

                    
Every tool in this guide is checked to make sure it works well with screen readers, captions, keyboard controls, and clear layouts so students with different learning needs can participate fully.
